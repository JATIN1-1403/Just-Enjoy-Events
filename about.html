Our story

Weddings, run the way family would run them.

We didn't set out to build an events company. We set out to make sure no couple in our family ever had to stress on their wedding day — and then friends started asking us to do the same for them.

How it started

From helping relatives to helping hundreds of couples

It started small — coordinating a cousin's wedding because "someone in the family had to keep track of everything." Word spread. Then it was a second wedding, then a friend of a friend, then couples we'd never met before who'd simply heard that this family gets it done without the drama.

Nine years and 50+ weddings later, that's still exactly what we do. We just do it on purpose now.

What we believe

Our Philosophy

🕊️

Your day, not our schedule

We build every timeline around what actually matters to you — not a template we reuse for every couple.

📋

Detail is the whole job

A wedding is hundreds of small decisions. Our job is to make almost none of them your problem.

🏡

Family, not vendors

We show up the way relatives would — invested, on time, and honest with you when it counts.

Our mission

Why we chose events

Because a wedding only happens once, and most of what people remember from a stressful one isn't the flowers — it's the panic. We exist to remove the panic. If we've done our job right, the only thing you remember is how it felt to celebrate.
